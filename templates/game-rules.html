🏏 CrickBet — Game Rules

Game Rules

General

  • Users must ensure their account information is correct.
  • Bets are accepted only when the selected market is available.
  • Once a bet is accepted, it may not be cancelled except according to the applicable market rules.

Odds

  • Displayed odds may change before a bet is accepted.
  • The odds shown at acceptance are the odds applicable to the bet.

Account Balance

  • Account balances are maintained by CrickBet.
  • Manual balance adjustments may be recorded in the account statement.

Responsible Betting

  • Only bet amounts you can afford to lose.
  • Contact support if you have an account or balance issue.
